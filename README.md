# Baboo Play Yard

Baboo Play Yard is a playful keyboard experience for young children ages 1 to 5.

The project is designed to let a child interact with a parent on the parent's computer in a safer, more child-friendly way. Instead of unrestricted computer use, Baboo Play Yard gives children a simple space where pressing keys can feel fun, engaging, and appropriate for their age.

## Purpose

Baboo Play Yard helps:

- give young children a fun keyboard-based activity
- encourage parent-child interaction during play
- create a safer environment for early computer exploration
- make the computer feel welcoming without exposing children to the full system

## Audience

- children ages 1 to 5
- parents or caregivers supervising play

## Project Goal

Build a simple, safe, and enjoyable keyboard play space for very young children using a parent's computer.

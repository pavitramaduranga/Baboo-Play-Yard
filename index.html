Press any letter or number to see big colorful feedback, playful sounds, and friendly emoji reactions. Baboo Play Yard is built for toddlers and preschoolers who want to explore a real keyboard while a parent or caregiver stays close.

Parents Often Ask

Is Baboo Play Yard for toddlers?

Yes. It is designed for young children ages 1 to 5 with simple keyboard play.

Does it work with a laptop keyboard?

Yes. Children can press real keyboard keys or click the on-screen keyboard.

Can parents turn sound off?

Yes. The keyboard play page includes a sound control for quieter play.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Baboo Play Yard is free to open and play in a browser.
